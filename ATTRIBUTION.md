# Attribution and source material

All animal meshes, underwater scenery and the favicon are procedural/original project code created with AI assistance. No external 3D models, animal photographs, textures or post-processing assets are used. Application screenshots, when present, show local project rendering.

Species evidence consists of concise factual paraphrases. Source publications are linked, not copied or relicensed. Complete metadata and record-level references are in `lib/corpus.ts` for:

- NOAA Fisheries: Atlantic salmon profile and facts, western Atlantic bluefin tuna, and green turtle.
- Smithsonian Ocean: giant squid size, anatomy and ecology.
- Florida Museum and Georgia Aquarium: whale shark.
- Natural History Museum, London: giant squid identity.
- Aquarium of the Pacific: moon jelly.

All corpus sources record an access date of 2026-09-12. Source ownership, trademarks and site terms remain with their publishers. No institutional endorsement is implied.

DM Sans and Manrope are optionally requested from Google Fonts at runtime under their respective open font licences. They are not copied into this repository; CSS supplies system fallbacks. Dependency licences remain with their packages and are recorded through the lockfile.
